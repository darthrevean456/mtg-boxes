MY CARD BOXES WEBSITE — HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, with GitHub Pages)

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html   - home page with all your boxes
  box.html     - the card page for one box
  app.js, style.css, boxes.js - the code that runs the pages
  data/        - your ManaBox exports (red.csv, white.csv, blue.csv, green.csv)

---------------------------------------------------------------
PUT IT ONLINE (one time, about 10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to github.com and make a free account.
2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository".
   - Name it:  mtg-boxes
   - Tick "Public", then click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file".
   Drag in EVERYTHING from this website folder, including the data folder.
   Click "Commit changes".
4. Go to Settings > Pages.
   Under "Branch" pick "main" and "/ (root)", then click Save.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your site will be at:
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mtg-boxes/

YOUR QR CODE LINKS (one per box):
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mtg-boxes/box.html?b=red
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mtg-boxes/box.html?b=white
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mtg-boxes/box.html?b=blue
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mtg-boxes/box.html?b=green
  -> Send these to Claude and it will make the QR labels.

---------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING CARDS TO A BOX LATER
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Add the cards to that box's list in ManaBox and export it again.
2. Rename the export to match: red.csv / white.csv / blue.csv / green.csv
3. On GitHub, open the "data" folder > "Add file" > "Upload files",
   drag the new file in (it replaces the old one) > "Commit changes".
4. Done. The QR label on the box stays the same.

ADDING A NEW BOX (e.g. RED 2 or BLACK 1)
- Put its export in the data folder (e.g. black.csv).
- Open boxes.js and copy one line, changing the id, name, colour and file.
- Upload both to GitHub.

NOTES
- Card pictures and text come from Scryfall automatically.
- If you open box.html straight from your laptop (not online), it will ask
  you to choose the CSV file. That's normal; online it loads by itself.
